# Milestone and Phase Boundary Review

## Purpose and sources

Use this optional checklist to inspect whether individual work results support a shared outcome. It applies the [Repository Milestone Standard](../standards/repository-milestones.md), relevant [lifecycle document](../standards/master-execution-checklist.md#operational-inventory), [Definition of Done](../standards/definition-of-done.md), and [Work Management](../standards/work-management.md).

A milestone can span phases or represent only part of one. Review its declared boundary; do not equate every milestone with a phase or require milestones for products that do not use them. This practice creates no extra mandatory ceremony or approval.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Identify the outcome, scope, owner, included-work rule, and completion criteria from the actual milestone or equivalent bounded record.
- [ ] Establish the reviewed source revision and current tracking snapshot. Confirm the complete selected set of included items, including relevant open work, rather than assuming a first page or filtered view is complete.
- [ ] Trace each completion criterion to evidence of the combined outcome. Review relevant item results, decisions, validation, and integration evidence; a closed status alone is insufficient.
- [ ] Compare maintained documents, work records, milestone scope, and material decisions. Resolve conflicting claims about current scope, ownership, completion, or pending work using their governing sources.
- [ ] Check dependencies between items: can the combined result support its intended use? Identify gaps or conflicting assumptions that individual acceptance checks missed.
- [ ] Record open obligations, risks, exclusions, and exceptions with the treatment required by the applicable standards. Explain whether they prevent this boundary's completion.
- [ ] Record the boundary outcome and rationale against its own criteria. Update the tracking representation only to reflect the supported decision.
- [ ] If progression is proposed, separately assess the relevant phase exit criteria and next activity's inputs, readiness, Assignments, and authorization.

When evidence is missing or observations are incomplete, retain that limit and identify the next check. Do not infer successful completion or an authorized next action.

## Compact review record

The following is an optional record shape, reusable in an existing issue, decision record, or review:

- Boundary and intended outcome:
- Accountable owner and applicable reviewer/decision Authority:
- Source revision, tracking snapshot, included set, and collection limits:
- Criterion → evidence → finding:
- Cross-record inconsistencies and disposition:
- Remaining obligations, owners, impact, and reassessment conditions:
- Completion determination and rationale:
- Separate next-entry assessment and resulting work:

The evidence and decision responsibilities remain with their governing sources. A milestone review is not release publication, independent approval, or certification of an entire product.

## Example of a finding

All Foundation issues are closed, but the workflow document says manual-only execution while the conformance declaration claims autonomous changes are enabled. Inspect the current adopted decision and its evidence, reconcile the documents and tracking records, and reassess the affected criteria. Neither the closed issues nor the milestone's percentage resolves the inconsistency.
