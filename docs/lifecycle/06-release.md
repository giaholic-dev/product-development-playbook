# 06 — Release

## Purpose

Make an integrated product increment available to its intended users with a clear scope, release record, and recovery path.

## Inputs

- An integrated increment that meets the applicable [Definition of Done](../standards/definition-of-done.md).
- Required validation, review, and operational evidence.
- Identified release scope, risks, and affected users or operators.
- The adopted versioning, deployment, and recovery decisions.

## Activities

- **R1 Release planning:** Select the increment, intended audience, versioning approach, and release conditions.
- **R2 Release readiness:** Confirm the required evidence, known limitations, communications, and recovery path.
- **R3 Publication:** Make the approved increment available through the adopted release and deployment process.
- **R4 Communication and observation:** Publish release information, observe the initial outcome, and record follow-up work.
- **R5 Recovery:** Correct, mitigate, or roll back when release evidence shows that the intended outcome is not being achieved.

## Outputs and Artifacts

- A released product increment and its version or equivalent release identifier.
- Release notes that state the scope, relevant changes, known limitations, and required action.
- Release, deployment, observation, and recovery evidence appropriate to the change.
- Traceable follow-up work for accepted risks, defects, or deferred improvements.

## Definition of Done

Release work is complete when the intended increment is available to its target audience, its release information and evidence are recorded, and any remaining risk has an accountable follow-up or explicit acceptance.

## Exit Criteria

Maintenance work may begin when the released increment has an identified operational owner, a means to receive and assess feedback or incidents, and traceable follow-up work for known obligations.
