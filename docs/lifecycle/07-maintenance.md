# 07 — Maintenance

## Purpose

Sustain a released product through observation, support, correction, learning, and intentional evolution.

## Inputs

- Released increments and their operational ownership.
- Feedback, support requests, defects, incidents, and product or technical signals.
- Applicable release, recovery, repository, and product decisions.

## Activities

- **M1 Observe and assess:** Receive and assess product, user, operational, and technical signals.
- **M2 Respond and recover:** Coordinate support, incident response, defect correction, or recovery work proportionate to impact.
- **M3 Improve and evolve:** Convert validated learning, risks, debt, and opportunities into traceable work.
- **M4 Preserve operational knowledge:** Keep relevant ownership, decisions, evidence, and product documentation current.

## Outputs and Artifacts

- Assessed feedback, incidents, defects, and operational signals.
- Corrective, preventive, and improvement work with accountable ownership.
- Updated operational, release, product, or architecture evidence when applicable.
- Decisions that may initiate a new lifecycle increment or revisit an earlier phase.

## Definition of Done

Maintenance work is complete when the assessed need has an evidenced outcome, an accountable follow-up, or an explicitly accepted decision. Work that changes product scope, experience, or architecture MUST return to the relevant earlier phase.

## Exit Criteria

Maintenance is continuous for an active product. A maintenance increment exits when its outcome is recorded and any resulting lifecycle work is traceable.
