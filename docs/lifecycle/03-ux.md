# 03 — UX

## Purpose

Transform the Product definition into a validated experience direction that can inform Architecture and Development without prematurely prescribing implementation.

## Inputs

- Product Vision, Problem Statement, Users, Outcomes, Product Scope, and Product Backlog.
- Relevant evidence, constraints, and assumptions from the Product phase.

## Activities

- **U1 UX Vision:** Define the intended experience direction in support of Product Vision and Outcomes.
- **U2 User Understanding:** Refine relevant user context through research, journeys, personas, or another appropriate representation.
- **U3 Experience Structure:** Define user flows and information architecture needed to make the intended experience understandable.
- **U4 Experience Exploration:** Create wireframes, prototypes, or other representations proportionate to the decisions being tested.
- **U5 UX Validation:** Gather and evaluate evidence about the proposed experience, then record resulting decisions or follow-up work.

## Outputs and Artifacts

- A documented UX direction.
- Evidence-based user and context understanding.
- Experience flows and structural decisions.
- Representations of the proposed experience.
- Validation findings and resulting decisions.

The project retains only the artifacts needed to evidence these outputs. A journey, persona, wireframe, or prototype is a possible representation, not a mandatory artifact for every product.

## Definition of Done

UX is complete when evidence exists that:

- the intended experience supports the current Product Vision, Problem Statement, Users, Outcomes, and Scope;
- the material user interactions and information structure are sufficiently understood for the next lifecycle decision;
- significant experience assumptions have been validated or recorded as explicit risk;
- the selected experience artifacts and decisions are traceable; and
- resulting work is reflected in the Product Backlog or linked follow-up items.

## Exit Criteria

The Architecture phase may begin when it can use the documented experience direction, material interaction constraints, and recorded validation evidence. Unresolved UX questions MAY remain only when their owner, impact, and reassessment condition are recorded and they do not prevent the architectural decision at hand.
