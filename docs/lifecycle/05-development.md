# 05 — Development

## Purpose

Implement, integrate, and validate product work in small, traceable increments consistent with the current Product, UX, and Architecture decisions.

## Inputs

- Ready work items from the Product Backlog.
- Applicable Product, UX, Architecture, and operational constraints.
- Adopted repository workflow, review, completion, coding, testing, and integration standards.

## Activities

- **D1 Implementation:** Build the selected work increment within its documented scope and constraints.
- **D2 Verification:** Apply the validation appropriate to the change's risk and affected behavior.
- **D3 Review and Integration:** Record review evidence and integrate the change through the adopted workflow.
- **D4 Learning and Refinement:** Record findings, defects, debt, or follow-up work and update relevant artifacts.

## Outputs and Artifacts

- Integrated implementation increments.
- Validation and review evidence.
- Updated source, configuration, tests, automation, and documentation when applicable.
- Traceable follow-up work for known limitations, defects, risks, or debt.

## Definition of Done

Development work is complete when its stated outcome and acceptance evidence meet the [Definition of Done](../standards/definition-of-done.md), required review and validation evidence are recorded, and integration satisfies [Branch Workflow](../standards/branch-workflow.md).

## Exit Criteria

Release work may begin when the selected increment is integrated, has the required release evidence, and any unresolved risk is explicitly accepted or represented by linked follow-up work.
