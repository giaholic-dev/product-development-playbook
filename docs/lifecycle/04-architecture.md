# 04 — Architecture

## Purpose

Transform validated product and experience decisions into an intentional technical structure that can guide development without selecting unnecessary technology.

## Inputs

- Current Product and UX artifacts, decisions, constraints, and risks.
- Applicable operational, security, quality, and integration requirements.

## Activities

- Define the system boundaries, responsibilities, interfaces, data, and quality attributes relevant to the product.
- Evaluate material technical options and record architecture decisions.
- Identify implementation constraints, risks, and validation required before development.
- Refine the Product Backlog with architecture-enabling work where needed.

## Outputs and Artifacts

- An architecture direction and material decision records.
- Documented system boundaries, interfaces, data, and quality constraints.
- Traceable technical risks, assumptions, and validation evidence.
- Architecture-enabling work linked to the Product Backlog.

## Definition of Done

Architecture is complete when the material technical decisions, constraints, and risks are sufficiently evidenced for the intended development decision. Unresolved uncertainty MAY remain only with a recorded owner, impact, and reassessment condition.

## Exit Criteria

Development may begin when the selected architecture provides enough direction to implement and validate the next planned work without treating all future design decisions as fixed.
