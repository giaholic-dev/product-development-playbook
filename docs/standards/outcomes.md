# Outcomes

## Purpose

Define what Outcomes are and establish their role within the Product Development Playbook.

## Scope

This document defines the concept of Outcomes independently of any specific product management framework or methodology.

## Definition

Outcomes are the desired changes resulting from the successful use of a product.

They describe the improvements the product intends to create for users, the business, or other stakeholders.

## Conceptual Model

Outcomes define the success criteria for a product independently of the specific solutions used to achieve them.

They provide a bridge between understanding the problem and defining the product scope.

Outcomes guide prioritization and decision-making throughout the product lifecycle.

Outcomes do not prescribe features, implementation details, or delivery plans.

## Characteristics

Outcomes should be:

- Meaningful
- User- and business-oriented
- Measurable whenever appropriate
- Independent of specific solutions
- Aligned with the Product Vision
- Derived from the identified problem and users

## Relationships

Outcomes are defined based on the understanding of the identified problem and its users.

They establish the desired future state that the product seeks to achieve.

As the Product phase progresses, outcomes guide the definition of product scope and the creation of the initial product backlog.
