# Outcomes

## Purpose

Define the desired changes that demonstrate the value a product intends to create.

## Scope

This document defines Outcomes independently of any measurement framework or delivery method.

## Definition

Outcomes are desired changes for users, customers, the business, operations, enabling capabilities, or risk exposure. They may result from product use, a changed operating condition, a validated decision, or another relevant product effect.

## Conceptual Model

Outcomes define success independently of features, implementation details, or delivery plans. They connect the problem and relevant actors to Product Scope and later prioritization.

An outcome MUST identify the change sought and the affected actor, system, or condition. It MUST identify evidence appropriate to the decision: a measure, observable signal, qualitative finding, or explicit rationale for why direct measurement is not yet practical. The evidence need not be a fixed metric when the outcome is exploratory or early-stage.

## Characteristics

Outcomes SHOULD be meaningful, solution-independent, aligned with Product Vision, and derived from the Problem Statement and relevant actors.

## Relationships

Outcomes guide Product Scope and Product Backlog decisions. They MAY be revised as evidence changes, while the Product Vision remains the durable direction.
