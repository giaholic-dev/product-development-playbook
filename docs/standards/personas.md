# Personas

## Purpose

Define a persona as an optional representation that helps a team make experience decisions about a meaningful user group.

## Scope

This standard is independent of persona template, research method, or design framework.

## Definition

A Persona is an evidence-informed, purpose-specific representation of a user group with relevant goals, behaviors, contexts, and needs. It is not a fictional substitute for evidence or a mandatory artifact.

## Rules

A persona MUST state the user group, intended decision use, and supporting evidence or assumptions. It MUST be revised or retired when material evidence changes. A persona MUST NOT represent customers, buyers, stakeholders, or operators as users unless they directly interact with the product in the relevant context.

Use a persona only when it improves a decision. Another representation may be used when it better fits the available evidence.

## Relationships

Personas refine the Users standard and may inform UX Vision, User Journeys, User Flows, validation, and Product Backlog refinement.
