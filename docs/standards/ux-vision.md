# UX Vision

## Purpose

Define the intended quality and character of the product experience in support of the Product Vision and desired Outcomes.

## Scope

This document defines UX Vision independently of a design system, interface technology, or specific design method.

## Definition

A UX Vision describes the experience users and other relevant actors should have while pursuing their goals with the product. It expresses the intended experience qualities, principles, and meaningful experience outcomes without prescribing a specific interface solution.

## Conceptual Model

UX Vision translates the Product Vision, Problem Statement, Users, and Outcomes into experience direction. It guides journeys, flows, information architecture, prototypes, and validation.

It MUST be revisited when material user evidence, product direction, or constraints change. It does not replace Product Vision or define implementation architecture.

## Characteristics

A UX Vision SHOULD be clear, actor-centered, outcome-oriented, evidence-informed, and usable for evaluating experience decisions.
