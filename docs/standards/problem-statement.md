# Problem Statement

## Purpose

Define the problem a product intends to address.

## Scope

This document defines Problem Statement independently of any product management framework or delivery method.

## Definition

A Problem Statement describes the current situation, the meaningful gap, pain, risk, or missed opportunity, and the context needed to make product decisions.

## Conceptual Model

The Problem Statement translates Product Vision into a concrete problem space. It establishes a shared understanding before selecting outcomes, scope, or solutions.

It MUST NOT prescribe a solution, implementation approach, feature, or delivery plan. Its evidence MAY be qualitative or quantitative, but it SHOULD make clear what is observed, assumed, or still uncertain.

## Characteristics

A Problem Statement SHOULD be clear, specific, evidence-informed, understandable to relevant actors, and solution-independent.

## Relationships

The Problem Statement refines the Product Vision and informs Users, Outcomes, Product Scope, and the initial Product Backlog.
