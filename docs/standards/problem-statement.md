# Problem Statement

## Purpose

Define what a Problem Statement is and establish its role within the Product Development Playbook.

## Scope

This document defines the concept of Problem Statement independently of any specific product management framework or methodology.

## Definition

A Problem Statement is a clear description of the problem that the product intends to solve.

It establishes the current situation, identifies the gap or pain that exists, and provides the context necessary to guide product decisions.

## Conceptual Model

The Problem Statement translates the strategic direction established by the Product Vision into a concrete problem space.

It provides a shared understanding of the problem before users, outcomes, scope, or solutions are defined.

The Problem Statement does not prescribe solutions, implementation approaches, or product features.

## Characteristics

A Problem Statement should be:

- Clear
- Specific
- Evidence-based
- User-centered
- Solution-independent
- Understandable by all stakeholders

## Relationships

The Problem Statement refines the strategic direction established by the Product Vision.

It provides the foundation for the remaining concepts defined during the Product phase.

As the Product phase progresses, the identified problem is progressively refined into users, outcomes, product scope, and ultimately the initial product backlog.
