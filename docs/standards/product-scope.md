# Product Scope

## Purpose

Define the bounded product focus selected to address the problem and pursue intended outcomes.

## Scope

This document defines Product Scope independently of implementation architecture, delivery planning, or project management methodology.

## Definition

Product Scope identifies the capabilities, user interactions, operating contexts, and outcome opportunities that the product will address in the current product boundary. It also records intentional exclusions from that boundary.

The underlying problem, relevant actors, and desired outcomes remain context even when a particular capability or segment is out of scope. An exclusion means the current product boundary does not address it; it does not deny that the context exists or that a later decision may revisit it.

## Conceptual Model

Product Scope translates the Problem Statement, Users, and Outcomes into a bounded definition of what the product is expected to address. It does not define implementation details, technical architecture, delivery sequence, or a complete project plan.

## Characteristics

Product Scope SHOULD be explicit about inclusions and exclusions, outcome-driven, consistent with Product Vision, and revised only through an intentional decision supported by evidence.

## Relationships

Product Scope provides the basis for creating and refining the Product Backlog.
