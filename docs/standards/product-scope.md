# Product Scope

## Purpose

Define what Product Scope is and establish its role within the Product Development Playbook.

## Scope

This document defines the concept of Product Scope independently of any specific product management framework or methodology.

## Definition

Product Scope defines the boundaries of the product.

It identifies which capabilities, problems, users, and outcomes are intentionally included within the product, as well as those intentionally excluded.

## Conceptual Model

Product Scope transforms the desired outcomes into a bounded definition of the product.

It establishes the limits of what the product is expected to address before implementation planning begins.

Product Scope does not define implementation details, technical architecture, delivery sequencing, or project planning.

## Characteristics

Product Scope should be:

- Clearly bounded
- Outcome-driven
- Consistent with the Product Vision
- Derived from the identified problem and users
- Explicit about inclusions and exclusions
- Stable throughout a product iteration unless intentionally revised

## Relationships

Product Scope refines the desired outcomes into a bounded product definition.

As the Product phase progresses, the defined scope provides the basis for creating the initial product backlog.
