# Definition of Done

## Purpose

Define the minimum evidence that a work item or change has achieved its intended completion state.

## Completion Rule

A work item is done when its stated outcome or decision has been achieved, the relevant acceptance evidence has been reviewed, and any remaining risk is explicitly accepted or represented by a linked follow-up item.

A completed work item MUST have:

- evidence that its stated outcome, acceptance condition, or research decision was addressed;
- the validation appropriate to its risk and affected artifacts;
- any required documentation, operational information, or artifact updates;
- traceability to the change or decision record when one exists; and
- a recorded disposition for known limitations, follow-up work, or exceptions.

A research item is done when it produces the evidence or decision it set out to obtain; it is not required to deliver production functionality.

## Integration Completion

When work changes a repository, integration completion also requires the review evidence and default-branch conditions defined by [Code Review Standard](code-review-standard.md) and [Branch Workflow](branch-workflow.md).

## Exceptions

A project MAY declare work done with deferred obligations only when the accountable owner records the deferred item, risk, reason, and reassessment condition. Deferred work does not erase the original acceptance evidence.
