# Architecture Decision Records

## Purpose

Define how material architecture decisions are recorded so their context, rationale, and consequences remain traceable.

## Scope

This standard applies to architecture decisions independently of a specific record template or repository platform.

## Definition

An Architecture Decision Record (ADR) is a durable record of a material architectural decision, its context, selected option, rationale, consequences, and status.

## Rules

An ADR MUST identify the decision, context, alternatives considered when meaningful, decision rationale, consequences, owner, and status. It MUST link to relevant evidence, constraints, or work items when they exist.

An ADR MAY be amended, superseded, or retired; the relationship and reason MUST remain traceable. An ADR does not replace implementation specifications or routine change history.
