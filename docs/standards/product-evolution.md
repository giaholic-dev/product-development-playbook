# Product Evolution

## Purpose

Define how a product turns sustained evidence into intentional changes while preserving traceability to the lifecycle decisions that govern the work.

## Scope

Product evolution covers material changes to product outcomes, users, scope, experience, architecture, and operating assumptions. It does not replace routine defect correction, incident response, or a delivery workflow.

## Evolution Decision

A proposed evolution MUST identify the observed evidence or opportunity, intended outcome, affected users or obligations, constraints, accountable decision-maker, and the lifecycle phase that must be revisited.

Changes to product intent or scope MUST return to Product. Changes to experience or interaction behavior MUST return to UX. Changes to technical structure or constraints MUST return to Architecture. Implementation proceeds through Development and Release only after the applicable earlier-phase evidence is updated.

## Evidence and Learning

Link evolution decisions to the relevant monitoring, feedback, incident, defect, debt, experiment, or delivery evidence. Record accepted assumptions and the conditions that would require reassessment.
