# Definition of Ready

## Purpose

Define the minimum evidence that makes a work item ready to begin without pretending that every uncertainty has been removed.

## Scope

This standard applies to work items before responsible work begins. It does not prescribe delivery methodology, estimation, or workflow states.

## Readiness Rule

A work item is ready when the accountable owner can make an informed start decision and the team has enough shared understanding to perform the next planned work safely.

A ready work item MUST identify:

- the intended outcome or problem to address;
- its primary Lifecycle Phase and Work Type;
- the accountable owner or decision-maker;
- the relevant scope, constraints, dependencies, and assumptions known at the time;
- the acceptance evidence or decision that will demonstrate progress or completion; and
- the next action that can begin.

Information MAY be proportionate to the item’s risk and size. Research items may be ready with a question, a decision boundary, and an evidence-gathering approach; they do not require an implementation specification.

## Not Ready

A work item is not ready when a missing decision, dependency, access, safety constraint, or essential acceptance condition prevents a responsible start. Record the blocker and the owner of the next decision rather than filling gaps with assumed detail.

## Exceptions

A project MAY start work before all readiness evidence is available only for an emergency, time-sensitive discovery, or explicitly accepted risk. The exception MUST record what is missing, why starting is justified, who accepts the risk, and when readiness will be reassessed.
