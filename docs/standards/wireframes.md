# Wireframes

## Purpose

Define Wireframes as low- to mid-fidelity representations used to evaluate structure, content, interaction, and hierarchy before implementation.

## Scope

This standard is independent of design tooling, visual style, and implementation technology.

## Definition

A Wireframe represents an intended interface or interaction at the level of structure and behavior needed for a decision. It is not a production design or a complete visual specification.

## Rules

A wireframe MUST identify the decision, actor or flow, scope, and evidence or assumptions it represents. It SHOULD show the information, controls, states, and hierarchy needed to evaluate that decision.

Wireframes MAY be sketches, diagrams, annotated screens, or another representation. They are created when they reduce a material UX, product, or architecture uncertainty.

## Relationships

Wireframes are informed by UX Vision, User Flows, UX Information Architecture, and validation evidence. They may inform prototypes, Architecture, and Product Backlog refinement.
