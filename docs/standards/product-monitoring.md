# Product Monitoring

## Purpose

Define how a product observes evidence about product outcomes, user experience, reliability, and operational health to support timely, accountable decisions.

## Scope

Monitoring includes quantitative and qualitative signals. It does not prescribe analytics platforms, dashboards, service-level targets, or data-collection technology.

## Monitoring Design

A product MUST identify the decisions its monitoring supports, the relevant signals, accountable owners, review cadence or trigger, and limits on interpretation. Signals MAY include user feedback, outcome measures, behavior, reliability, support demand, incidents, and delivery or quality trends.

Monitoring MUST respect applicable privacy, security, and data-governance constraints.

## Assessment and Action

A signal MUST be assessed in context before it drives a decision. When evidence indicates material product, operational, or user impact, create or update traceable work, an incident, or an earlier-phase decision as appropriate.

Monitoring records SHOULD distinguish observed facts, interpretation, assumptions, and planned actions.
