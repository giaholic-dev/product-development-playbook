# Product Vision

## Purpose

Define the durable direction a product is intended to pursue.

## Scope

This document defines Product Vision independently of any product management framework or delivery method.

## Definition

A Product Vision is a high-level description of the long-term direction of a product. It explains why the product exists, the value it intends to create, and the future state it aims to achieve.

## Conceptual Model

The Product Vision guides strategic product decisions, discovery, prioritization, and long-term evolution. It does not describe implementation details, delivery plans, or technical solutions.

The vision is durable direction, not immutability. It MUST be revisited when material evidence changes the problem, intended value, relevant actors, constraints, or strategic context. A revision records the evidence and decision without treating ordinary learning as loss of direction.

## Characteristics

A Product Vision SHOULD be clear, inspiring, long-term, outcome-oriented, and independent of a proposed solution.

## Relationships

The Product Vision provides strategic direction for the Problem Statement, Users, Outcomes, Product Scope, and Product Backlog.
