# Product Vision

## Purpose

Define what a Product Vision is and establish its role within the Product Development Playbook.

## Scope

This document defines the concept of Product Vision independently of any specific product management framework or methodology.

## Definition

A Product Vision is a high-level description of the long-term direction of a product.

It explains why the product exists, the value it intends to create, and the future state it aims to achieve.

## Conceptual Model

The Product Vision guides strategic product decisions throughout the product lifecycle.

It provides direction for product discovery, prioritization, and long-term product evolution.

The Product Vision does not describe implementation details, delivery plans, or technical solutions.

## Characteristics

A Product Vision should be:

- Clear
- Inspiring
- Long-term
- User-centered
- Outcome-oriented
- Stable over time

## Relationships

The Product Vision provides strategic direction for the remaining concepts defined during the Product phase.

As the Product phase progresses, these concepts progressively refine the Product Vision into actionable product work.
