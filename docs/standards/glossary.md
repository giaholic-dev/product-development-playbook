# Glossary

## Purpose

Provide a discoverable catalog of approved cross-cutting terms used by the Product Development Playbook.

The primary owner of each term remains the linked lifecycle document or standard. This catalog aids navigation and does not redefine those concepts.

| Term | Definition | Primary owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accountability | Answerability for a material responsibility or decision within a defined scope. | [Roles and Actor Assignment Standard](roles-and-actor-assignment.md) |
| Accountable Owner | Role holding required accountability within an applicable scope. | [Roles and Actor Assignment Standard](roles-and-actor-assignment.md) |
| Actor | Entity that performs or participates in Playbook work. | [Roles and Actor Assignment Standard](roles-and-actor-assignment.md) |
| Approver | Role responsible for a governed approval decision within an applicable scope. | [Roles and Actor Assignment Standard](roles-and-actor-assignment.md) |
| Artifact | An inspectable record, deliverable, or durable result created, adopted, or materially changed by product work. | [Artifact Standard](artifact-standard.md) |
| Assignment | Relationship through which an Actor fulfills a Role within a defined scope. | [Roles and Actor Assignment Standard](roles-and-actor-assignment.md) |
| Authority | Explicit permission or decision right within a defined scope. | [Roles and Actor Assignment Standard](roles-and-actor-assignment.md) |
| Contributor | Role for performing or contributing work within an applicable scope. | [Roles and Actor Assignment Standard](roles-and-actor-assignment.md) |
| Definition of Done | The evidence required to consider a work item or outcome complete. | [Definition of Done](definition-of-done.md) |
| Definition of Ready | The minimum evidence that permits a responsible start. | [Definition of Ready](definition-of-ready.md) |
| Delegation | Explicit transfer of work performance within a defined scope. | [Roles and Actor Assignment Standard](roles-and-actor-assignment.md) |
| Lifecycle Phase | One ordered part of the Product Development Playbook lifecycle. | [Foundation Phase](../lifecycle/01-foundation.md) |
| Release | The phase that makes an integrated increment available with a release record and recovery path. | [Release Phase](../lifecycle/06-release.md) |
| Reviewer | Role responsible for evaluating governed work or evidence within an applicable scope. | [Roles and Actor Assignment Standard](roles-and-actor-assignment.md) |
| Role | Responsibility within Playbook execution. | [Roles and Actor Assignment Standard](roles-and-actor-assignment.md) |
| Work Item | A traceable unit of work classified with the adopted taxonomy and type model. | [Work Item Taxonomy](work-item-taxonomy.md) |
| Work Type | The nature of work being performed, distinct from its lifecycle phase and workflow state. | [Work Item Types](work-item-types.md) |

## Scope

This catalog covers approved cross-cutting vocabulary and links to its primary owners. It does not redefine terms or replace their governing standards.

## Maintenance

Add or change a term through its primary owner. Update this catalog when the approved vocabulary or its discoverability changes.
