# UX Validation

## Purpose

Define how experience assumptions and proposed interactions are evaluated with evidence before later lifecycle decisions rely on them.

## Scope

This standard is independent of research method, participant count, tooling, and product type.

## Definition

UX Validation is the deliberate collection and evaluation of evidence about whether an intended experience supports relevant actor goals, product outcomes, and constraints.

## Rules

A validation activity MUST state the question or assumption, the relevant actors or evidence source, the method or rationale, findings, limitations, and resulting decision. The method MUST be proportionate to the risk and uncertainty.

Validation MAY use observation, usability evaluation, feedback, analytics, expert review, accessibility review, prototype evaluation, or another appropriate method. Lack of validation evidence is a recorded risk, not implicit confirmation.

## Relationships

UX Validation evaluates UX Vision, journeys, flows, information architecture, wireframes, and prototypes. It informs Product Backlog refinement and UX exit decisions.
