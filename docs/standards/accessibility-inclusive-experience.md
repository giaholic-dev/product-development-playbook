# Accessibility and Inclusive Experience Standard

## Purpose

Define how product work assesses and addresses accessibility and inclusive-experience needs proportionate to relevant users, contexts, and risk.

## Scope

This standard applies to product decisions, experiences, technical constraints, validation, release, and maintenance where accessibility or inclusion may materially affect an actor. It does not prescribe a standard, tool, test method, or provider.

## Assessment and Evidence

A project MUST identify relevant accessibility and inclusion needs, accountable owner, evidence approach, and reassessment trigger when they may materially affect a product outcome. Evidence MAY include user research, expert review, prototype evaluation, automated checks, or another proportionate method.

## Decisions and Exceptions

A material accessibility decision, known limitation, or exception MUST record its impact, owner, rationale, evidence, follow-up, and reassessment condition. A project MAY determine that a concern is inapplicable when it records the rationale.

## Integration

Users and Product Scope identify relevant contexts; UX Validation evaluates experience evidence; Architecture and Testing address applicable constraints; Release and Maintenance preserve known limitations and follow-up work.
