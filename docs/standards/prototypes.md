# Prototypes

## Purpose

Define Prototypes as representations used to test or communicate an experience hypothesis before production implementation.

## Scope

This standard is independent of prototype fidelity, tooling, and implementation technology.

## Definition

A Prototype simulates enough of an intended experience, interaction, or behavior to evaluate a stated question. It is evidence-gathering material, not production functionality.

## Rules

A prototype MUST state the hypothesis or decision it tests, intended participants or evaluators, boundary, and success or learning evidence. Its fidelity MUST be proportionate to the uncertainty it reduces.

A prototype MAY use static, interactive, coded, or other representations. It MUST NOT be treated as an implementation commitment merely because it is functional.

## Relationships

Prototypes are informed by UX Vision, User Flows, UX Information Architecture, and Wireframes. Their findings inform UX Validation, Product Backlog refinement, and later Architecture decisions.
