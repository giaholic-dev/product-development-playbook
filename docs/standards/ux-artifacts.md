# UX Artifacts

## Purpose

Define how a project selects and manages artifacts that evidence UX decisions without requiring a fixed set of design deliverables.

## Scope

This standard applies to UX artifacts and their relationship to the repository-wide Artifact Standard.

## Rules

A UX artifact MUST identify its decision or evidence purpose, scope, owner, and relationship to the UX work item or decision it supports. It MUST remain distinguishable from planned work and from implementation evidence.

A project MAY use journeys, personas, flows, information-architecture representations, wireframes, prototypes, validation records, specifications, or other appropriate artifacts. It retains only those needed to support traceability, collaboration, validation, or later lifecycle decisions.

The artifact category and subtype follow the [Artifact Standard](artifact-standard.md). A UX artifact does not become a mandatory phase output merely because it is a common design practice.

## Lifecycle

Create, update, supersede, or retire a UX artifact when its evidence or decision changes. Preserve a decision record or traceable history when the artifact's change materially affects Product Scope, Product Backlog, Architecture, or implementation.
