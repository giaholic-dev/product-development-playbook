# Master Execution Checklist Record

## Purpose

Provide reusable starting material for recording a bounded application of the [Master Execution Checklist](../docs/standards/master-execution-checklist.md). This template is non-normative and does not establish workflow states, a schema, or additional completion requirements.

## Scope and use

Identify the product increment or work item, selected source revision, applicable Assignments and Authority, and evidence locations. Repeat the activity record below for the applicable inventory entries. Replace instructional text with context-specific links or explicit absences, preserving the governing sources and their optionality. Record applicability decisions under existing governance rather than silently skipping a governing requirement.

The ten entry fields retain the checklist's navigational structure. The application evidence section records actual observations separately; copying an entry or checking a box is not proof of execution or Completion. No provider, integration, AI Actor, or machine representation is required.

## Application context

- Product increment or work item: identify the bounded scope and its source record.
- Source revision: identify the lifecycle and checklist revision used.
- Assignments and Authority: link to the applicable scoped records under the [Roles and Actor Assignment Standard](../docs/standards/roles-and-actor-assignment.md).
- Evidence locations: identify maintained artifacts or discoverable external evidence under the [Artifact Standard](../docs/standards/artifact-standard.md).

### Application evidence

- Execution context and result: link to the relevant attempt, observed result, and evidence under the [Execution Protocol](../docs/standards/execution-protocol.md); distinguish unavailable capability from unavailable Authority.
- Validation and review: identify what was examined, the result, and unresolved findings under [Work Management](../docs/standards/work-management.md) and [Code Review](../docs/standards/code-review-standard.md) where applicable.
- Completion and next action: record the scoped decision and evidence under the linked lifecycle criteria and [Definition of Done](../docs/standards/definition-of-done.md). Preserve accepted risks, follow-up ownership, and any Re-entry decision; do not infer these from an attempt result.
