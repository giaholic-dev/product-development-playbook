# Foundation Completion Example

## Context and limits

This fictional example illustrates a small portfolio repository completing Foundation. It is informed by practical questions from adopting projects, but it does not certify giaholic.com or any real milestone, issue, merge, or deployment.

Use the [Foundation Phase](../docs/lifecycle/01-foundation.md), [Foundation checklist entries](../docs/standards/master-execution-checklist.md#foundation), [Definition of Done](../docs/standards/definition-of-done.md), and [boundary review practice](../docs/practices/milestone-boundary-review.md). The evidence names below are illustrative records, not files or URLs claimed to exist.

Rita holds explicitly recorded Contributor and Accountable Owner Assignments for this bounded work, and a separate Reviewer Assignment for permitted solo review under the [Code Review Standard](../docs/standards/code-review-standard.md). Any required approval Authority is recorded separately under the [Roles and Actor Assignment Standard](../docs/standards/roles-and-actor-assignment.md). Authorship alone does not confer Authority.

## F2 from reading to closure

The selected item is “Adopt repository standards.” Its acceptance evidence is a current adoption record identifying the applicable baseline, local choices, accountable ownership, exceptions, and unresolved decisions.

1. Rita reads F2 and its governing Repository Standards, then follows the standards relevant to the adoption choices. She uses the conformance template as a starting aid.
2. She records English as the documentation language, the repository's purpose and structure, adopted standards and local mappings, release-based versioning, ownership, and unresolved decisions.
3. She validates the resulting record against the applicable criteria. A checklist of headings alone would not establish adoption.
4. She compares the intended scope with the actual change, checks affected links, and records a solo review with findings, limitations, and the integration decision. The change is then integrated through the adopted workflow.
5. She records completion evidence and checks the integrated revision before marking the item complete in the chosen tracker.

An illustrative completion note reads:

> Outcome: repository standards adoption is recorded in the conformance record at the reviewed integrated revision. Validation compared the record with the adopted baseline, checked local mappings and links, and found no blocking omission. Solo review and integration evidence are linked from this work record. Hosting selection remains outside this item's scope; Rita owns the question and will reassess it before deployment planning. F2 is complete for its stated scope. F3 and Foundation completion remain separate assessments.

A real note substitutes actual discoverable artifact references, validation results, review evidence, and source revision for these descriptions. A comment, merged change, or closed issue alone would not prove the stated outcome.

## Combined Foundation evidence

| Activity | Illustrative evidence examined | Observed capability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | Repository baseline and access check | The selected version-control and tracking capabilities are usable. |
| F2 | Reviewed, integrated conformance record | Applicable standards and local choices are adopted and traceable. |
| F3 | Classified work records and a usable planning view with scoped ownership | Product work can be created, classified, ordered, and tracked. |
| F4 | Workflow adoption record, readiness/review/completion sources, and deliberate manual-only choice | The next work can follow the adopted gates and authority boundaries. |
| F5 | Root entry point, language declaration, and documentation map with checked links | Current documents and their governing sources can be found. |

The records support the [Foundation Definition of Done](../docs/lifecycle/01-foundation.md#definition-of-done); their mere existence would not be enough.

## Boundary review and remaining questions

The selected milestone groups F1–F5 with the outcome “enable deliberate Product work.” Rita checks its actual included set and criteria against the integrated documentation and current work records. The evidence agrees on scope, ownership, workflow, and remaining decisions.

Hosting is unresolved. Its owner, impact, and reassessment condition are recorded; it does not prevent defining the initial portfolio product. By contrast, if the planning model lacked an owner or could not track Product work, that would be an unmet Foundation criterion and Rita would retain the incomplete determination until addressed.

For this fictional successful case, Rita records that the milestone's criteria and Foundation's Definition of Done are met and updates the selected tracking records. The review is recorded self-review, not independent approval.

## Separate Product entry decision

Rita then checks Foundation's exit criteria and Product's inputs: she can create, classify, document, and review Product work; the initial opportunity and available context are recorded; no remaining Foundation question prevents those activities. The first Product item is evaluated under the applicable readiness and execution conditions.

The resulting decision permits the relevant Product work in its authorized scope. It does not certify UX, Architecture, implementation, or publication, and it does not promise the future tasks or calendar apps merely because they were mentioned as possibilities.
